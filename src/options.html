SlopGuard

Folds away AI-bot replies under posts on X. Click any folded reply to bring it back.

Model

Filtering

90% is the default: it hides only clear cases. Lower it to catch more, at the risk of folding real people.

Trusted accounts

Accounts you marked "Not AI". Their replies are never hidden.

    Privacy

    Your key stays in this browser's local extension storage and is sent only to the provider you picked. For each reply, the provider receives the post's text and author handle and the reply's text and handle. Nothing else from X is sent. Verdicts are cached locally for 14 days so the same reply is never paid for twice.
